01 · The problem

Credit-risk engineering for people with limited formal history.

MicroScore began with a local question in Pavlodar, Kazakhstan: can a useful decision-support system surface repayment context without pretending that a model should make the final decision?

02 · Research changed the product

The most valuable result was a limitation.

An ablation test showed that the apparent strength of the synthetic model depended heavily on a late-payment proxy. That finding changed the architecture from automation to review support.

04 · Monte Carlo layer

Uncertainty belongs at portfolio level.

Seeded simulations compare policy outcomes under explicit shocks. They do not modify borrower scores and they are presented as scenario planning, not validated loss forecasts.

06 · Responsible-use boundary

A prototype can be ambitious and still be honest.

MicroScore demonstrates the engineering of a review system. It does not claim that synthetic Pavlodar data proves real repayment prediction or that the prototype is ready to process real borrower data.

Not claimedAutomated credit decisions

Every final decision remains with a trained human reviewer.

Not claimedLocal predictive validity

Real regional validation requires consented data and expert partnership.

Not claimedProduction readiness

Identity, transactional delivery, hosting, and privacy review remain pre-pilot gates.

Next responsible milestone Privacy-safe validation with local experts or an MFI partner.
