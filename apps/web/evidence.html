Uncertainty design

Observed
Portfolio outcomes depend on policy and macro assumptions, not only scores.

Explicit assumptions instead of false forecast certainty.

Lifecycle safety

Mutable records → terminal decision guards

Observed
A final lending record must not be silently rescored or reversed.
Choice
Enforce lifecycle capabilities and append decision history.
Tradeoff

Observed
Demo readiness is not permission to process real borrower data.

Aggregate identity, storage, delivery, privacy, and model gaps into a gate.

04 · Engineering reflection

What the project taught.

The strongest progress came from treating failed assumptions as design inputs instead of hiding them.

01

Measure before claiming

A high score on synthetic data is a question to investigate, not a result to market.

02

Boundaries are architecture

“Not production ready” becomes useful when identity, storage, and validation blockers are explicit.

03

Uncertainty has layers

Borrower scoring uncertainty and portfolio policy uncertainty require different tools and language.

04

Reviewability changes design

A system becomes more credible when another person can trace inputs, decisions, tests, and limitations.

05 · Current boundary
